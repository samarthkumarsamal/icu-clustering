### Important Replication Detail

For the $12$ laboratory variables, Paper does not use only one value. It calculates minimum, maximum, mean and standard deviation during the first $24$ hours. Therefore :
$$
\text{12 labs} \times \text{4 summary statistics} = \text{48 laboratory features}
$$
It also calculates the total count of laboratory measurements during the first $24$ hours.

For SpO2, Heart rate and Respiratory rate, paper preserves their longitudinal behavior using functional prinicipal components (FPCs) with PACE, rather than simply calculating one mean value.
